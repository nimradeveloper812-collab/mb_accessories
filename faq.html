Ordering & Payment Process

How do I place an order on this website?

It's simple and personal! When you browse our products, simply click "Order on WhatsApp" or add multiple items to your "Inquiry List". A pre-filled WhatsApp message with product details and prices will open. Our team will verify stock and coordinate your delivery address right in chat!

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD) across major cities in Pakistan, as well as Direct Bank Transfer / Raast / EasyPaisa / JazzCash upon request. You do not need to enter credit cards online.

How long does shipping take?

Orders inside Gujranwala are typically delivered same-day or next-day. For other cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, etc.), delivery takes 2 to 4 working days via reliable courier partners.

7-Days Checking & Replacement Warranty

What is the 7-Days Replacement Policy?

All electronic accessories (chargers, cables, power banks, earbuds, car holders) come with a 7-Day Checking Warranty. If the product arrives with a manufacturing defect or fails to perform as advertised, we will replace it free of charge.

What conditions are required for a replacement?

  • Product must be in original condition with box and accessories intact.
  • Defects caused by accidental physical breakage, water submergence, or customer misuse are not covered.
  • Contact our WhatsApp support with your order number/receipt and a short video demonstrating the fault.

Device Compatibility & Quality

How do I make sure a charger is safe for my phone?

All our chargers feature smart Power Delivery (PD 3.0) and Quick Charge protocols that automatically negotiate the ideal voltage for your specific phone (iPhone, Samsung, Xiaomi, etc.). They will never send more power than your device safely requests.

Do you offer wholesale pricing for retailers?

Yes! If you run a mobile shop or need corporate gifting, message our wholesale department on WhatsApp with your required quantities for tiered bulk discounts.
